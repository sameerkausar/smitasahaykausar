## Before you upload: which PDF are you allowed to post?

Worth two minutes, since a personal site is public and program directors read it.

- **Open access (CC BY) — post the final published PDF freely.** That covers the
  MDPI titles (*Biology*, *IJMS*, *Brain Sciences*, *Cells*), *Tracheostomy Journal*,
  and *Journal of Bioinformatics & Systems Biology*.
- **Subscription journals — usually the accepted manuscript, not the typeset PDF.**
  That is the case for the two *Molecular Psychiatry* papers. Springer Nature
  generally permits the author-accepted manuscript on a personal site after a
  6-month embargo. Check the specific policy on
  [Sherpa Romeo](https://v2.sherpa.ac.uk/romeo/) before posting.
- **Under review — post nothing** until it is accepted, unless it is on a preprint
  server. The DOI link is enough in the meantime.
- **Dissertation** — hers, and already public on OhioLINK. Safe to post.

When in doubt, the DOI link alone is always safe; the PDF is a convenience.
